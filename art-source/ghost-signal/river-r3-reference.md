# Reference river sunset

`river-r3-reference.png` is the original 1792 × 352 ungraded campaign sky from production release `GameSession-IMUH3e07.js`. It is the paint behind the upper-right “AFTER / RIVER” in [the archived board](../../../TankDelete-Concepts/replaced-gauntlet/showcase-replaced-r3.jpg); [the original 1920 × 1080 photograph](../../../TankDelete-Concepts/replaced-gauntlet/1080p/replaced-r3/river-scenic.png) includes the historical buildings, trees and inspection camera. The painting (not a crop of the photograph) was recovered from the retained release at `tmp/production-qa/ghost-signal-monsoon-KS0dPQ3g/images/textures/ghost-horizon-river.png`. Source SHA-256: `b850e26694e45d5407c00cb350bfc8c84ea72fa72f3f80b0275efdd2051f3583`. The similarly named current public texture is from a *later* release and is not the reference.

`scripts/bake-river-reference.cjs` registers this painting with the existing live sun, water and wet-road receivers, retains two closer sculpted shoulders from the intervening karst release, and restores the distant reference hills and clouds directly behind the burning settlement. Coordinate and angular-pigment checks are run by `scripts/check-crossing-sky.cjs` and `scripts/check-joined-dawn.cjs`. No gameplay camera, stationary sky prop, or extra sky draw call is used. The embassy has a separate storm panorama.

The current bake emits `ghost-river-umber-karst-v8.png`. Only the nearer rock mask differs from v7: its top colors are compressed toward ash and umber and the 90.6° spur has 28% less relief above its 9.5° foot. The new opening reveals the underlying R3 painting, not a new cloud patch. The adjoining 85.5° tooth, other summit heights, original sun, sky and remote hills are retained. `node scripts/check-river-umber.cjs` compares both textures at full resolution, audits the angular mask and checks the independent original paint in the exposed crescent. The immutable v7 input is kept; use `--v7` only to deliberately rebuild that historical release.
