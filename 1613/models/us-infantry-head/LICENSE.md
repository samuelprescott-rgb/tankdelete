# CC0 authored infantry head

The original human mesh, young-adult male morph and selected skin are CC0 1.0.
The complete CC0 legal text is in `LICENSE-CC0.txt`. Source URLs, per-file SHA-256
checksums, authorship, and the exact selected skin's license are recorded in
`provenance.json`.

- **MakeHuman hm08 base mesh** — its source header explicitly releases it under
  CC0 in September 2020. Copyright contacts at release: Data Collection AB,
  Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier.
- **MPFB caucasian-male-young target** — official MakeHuman Community data, covered
  by the explicit CC0 targets/assets statement in
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md . The GPL
  license for application code is separate; no application code is included in
  these model assets.
- **Aksel skin** — author Mindfront, asset `mindfront_aksel_skin`. Its individual
  entry in the official checked inventory explicitly states CC0:
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html . This is
  corroborated by `packs/skins02.json` inside the official archive; the selected
  record is copied into our provenance. It is not inferred from the license of
  unrelated community assets. Original asset page:
  http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/850 .

The head crop, unit/orientation conversion and reduced far LOD were prepared for
Tank Delete from these sources. The diffuse PNG is unmodified. Normal/specular
maps, other skins, the full source body and source download archive are not
included in the game. The small eye geometry is original programmatic geometry.
