# Virtual campaign, vehicle and embassy acceptance

Historical test record: the Relay/Vault progression below is retired. In the current campaign the bridge never changes levels. Clear the village, disable the tank guarding the rear hut, and operate the hut radio. See [Play this version](../README.md#play-this-version) for the current controls and checks.

Personal local checkout; no external file targets, purchase, deployment, credentials or push. The inherited 20:46 Mountain start imposes a conservative 22:16 stop; 12 shared review rounds maximum. A typecheck or a preferred still is not a performance or AAA verdict.

## Fixed references and success conditions

- [War Thunder — M48A1](https://warthunder.com/en/news/3751-development-m48a1-en/): use vehicle silhouette, planted running gear and visible material/impact differences to judge the tank.
- [Black Ops 7 Zombies — Treyarch](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2025/09/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-zombies-deep-dive): judge the distinct, readable round phases, the environment framing approaching enemies, and the player's ability to act on a threat.

Do not substitute a different reference because an iteration lost. A critical pass requires the actual retained local build at the named resolution, an earned campaign transition, measurable combat and no bridge to host files. Every critical criterion **and** a fresh integration critique must pass before calling the work complete. Variant art can be blind-scored; branded publisher imagery and different camera routes do not make for an honest blind gameplay comparison.

The project remains React 19 + Three.js r182 + React Three Fiber on Vite 7, with a Tauri 2 desktop shell. The normal chase camera follows the hull; mouse aims the turret and right-drag orbits. Shared meshes, instancing, painted/vertex materials, local art and seeded terrain remain in use. Folder portals and file huts form the daytime level; named archive buildings and timed wave state form the night defense. The mounted encounter simulates combat time, not art quality or human skill. The stress capture stages the opening waves only **after** proving the earned campaign route in Chrome; for all seven measured seconds there is no wave or actor mutation. System WebKit uses an isolated saved-replay fixture and is not a packaged desktop binary.

### What the revised game actually did

- The Vault run retained `old-atlas.pdf`, `archived-reel.mov`, and `catalog.csv`. An independent compiled-bundle test removed the reel first: the third-place replacement was `color-studies.psd`, not a hard-coded default selection. If only one or two files remain, the other buildings are ruins, not invisible targets.
- In the paired earned-Vault 60 Hz mounted run, the natural front runner triggered a Dispatch warning at 11.72 s. With the original five allies and no intervention, it damaged `catalog.csv` at 14.05 s (192/200); turning and firing once after the same warning stopped it at 12.70 s (200/200). This is a small, measurable loss and an actionable rescue, **not** a claim one raider can instantly end the level. The 20 Hz four-wave victory took 27.85 / 25.80 / 28.40 / 41.60 simulated combat seconds and used assisted aim and scripted driving. This is one successful route, not human difficulty or all-seed proof.
- The tank responds on the first input frame, takes 720 ms to reach its unchanged top speed and coasts 1.28 units in 320 ms. The old values were 240 ms, 0.22 units, and 55 ms respectively. Modal/focus reset still stops immediately. A mounted wheel regression confirms actual rotating pairs remain round and correctly placed after the hull stretch. The minimap's headings now agree with Three world-to-hull coordinates at 128 distinct angles/offsets; the pulsing ring marks the named door, not the whole scene.
- [Earlier vs revised front approaches, on the same live night route](../tmp/production-qa/hero-comparison-PJkOwb/wave-three-after-timed-window.png). The tower now has an opening against the sky; one flank has a stepped marquee and the opposite flank a low canopy. These are camera landmarks, not spawn markers. Lighting and the number of draw batches are unchanged.
- The moving uncrewed crawler has actual ray/triangle surface outcomes. A glancing hit reflects a harmless effect off the surface normal; a direct hit on a charged cell shows a bounded internal pulse. Two cell hits stop the vehicle and it later reboots. These are fictional arcade values; the controlled test checks 30/60/120 Hz and that no file behind the crawler receives the spent shot. Player armor still has scalar health, not detailed component simulation.

### Final review: keep the playable build, hold release sign-off

The fresh integration critic executed the compiled virtual store without host APIs, ran the mounted contact test, recomputed frame intervals from the retained traces, and inspected earned-gameplay and source-unlabelled, randomized art pairs. The revised bow, road wheels, cupola grip and faces won their comparison views; the improvement is less obvious at chase distance. Field faces still share the same angular shape, cloth is flatter than the vehicle finish, and at night the HUD conveys danger more effectively than the enemy silhouettes. The vehicle and horde references still win. This was not a double-blind test against a commercial game.
