### Measured gates (1080p, not art sign-off)

**R23 — integrated field-kit checkpoint.** Preview 1550, GameSession-DgpYHaSZ.js, signed local Workshop qHN7KW. Original hilltop hut survives the joined tall dawn range. The asymmetric scout satchel is attached at both ends of its diagonal sling; the field leader carries a pack and roll. The independently watched unaltered chase shows both moving heads for the last moments; its 9–12 s trace reports at least 42 px between the full silhouettes and 88 px below the HUD. Reduced live tracer uploads and prewarmed the *actual* text layouts and materials. The isolated WebKit shader diagnostic Fwhb3z recorded no new GL programs during the route. This did not establish two clean strikes.

**R24 — actual channel and wet footwork.** Preview 1551, GameSession-DQzVyufV.js; Workshop TWzfYz, retained. Widened the real near channel around the terrain under the river patrol, preserved the deck, three surveyed homes and close-up bank cameras. At the same 1920×1080 chase row y=900, 99 → 291 unobstructed water pixels; the full envelope includes dry ground and is not a width measurement of uninterrupted water. Opposing rain trees, both parked-assignment manifests, and old sky flank/new hut survive. Boss sole contact uses sculpted pads, ankle roll and two local apron responses rather than a sliding shadow. Improved burned annex fire profiles use actual negative space. Opaque world depth is discarded after the world and only before color-only postprocessing if no depth consumer is present. Independent R24 judgment: rain, flag, kit and the two-headed approach win over R14, but the river **beyond** the bridge still looks narrow, the summit reads as plain stripes, and the final boss lacks localized reflected light along its approach-facing planes. Supplementary portraits with a clearance offset disclose it in the capture receipt; the ordinary chase is not staged.
