### Independent closing decision

At approximately 00:16 Mountain on November 20, the **24 shared builder–critic rounds** were used, after approximately 1 h 50 min of the 3 h maximum. Final reviewer `final_release_board` approved the browser playtest on the retained Apple M5 build, and declined AAA/reference parity and packaged-native release approval. No final listening test, public deployment, install, remote push, host file operation or native performance approval.
