# Ghost Signal: one art target, one open section

**Standing art rule: the driver's seat decides.** Start and finish every graphics review with the unaltered third-person tank camera, at ordinary driving and engagement distances. Judge the scene in motion: can the player read the route, tell figures from the backdrop, see where the light comes from, and anticipate an attack? The river at dusk and the embassy in rain need their own light and atmosphere while belonging to the same game. Spend pixels on silhouettes, broad material/value groups, localized lighting, depth and motion before facial pores, individual stitches or fingertip detail. Ghost Signal and REPLACED are the visual references. Use close-ups to diagnose failures or to show an explicitly requested detail; an isolated model win never overrides a worse or unchanged driver's view.

Direction is owned in this ledger.
